## Mission (their framing)

Encode how experts actually think — decisions, tradeoffs, tool sequences, recovery from failure — into training data and environments so models can do professional work, not just generate plausible answers.

Thesis: compute and algorithms commoditize; **high-quality training data and environments** are the scarce differentiator.

## Founders (YC bios, condensed)
